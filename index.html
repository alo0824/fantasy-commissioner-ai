Fantasy Commissioner AI

An AI-powered assistant designed to help fantasy football commissioners automate league analysis, weekly content, and league entertainment.

What is Fantasy Commissioner AI?

Fantasy Commissioner AI is an early-stage project focused on making fantasy football leagues easier and more entertaining to manage.

The goal is to connect authorized fantasy league data to an AI-powered commissioner that can analyze league activity, create weekly reports, track season-long storylines, and generate entertaining content for league members.

Initial Features

Weekly Matchups

Automatically analyze weekly fantasy football matchups, scores, margins of victory, and team performances.

Weekly Awards

Identify standout performances, highest and lowest scoring teams, biggest blowouts, closest matchups, and other league awards.

Power Rankings

Generate data-driven weekly power rankings based on team performance, roster strength, schedule, and other factors.

AI Newsletter

Generate a weekly fantasy football newsletter featuring league stories, matchup recaps, awards, analysis, and friendly banter.

Future Vision

  • Season-long league storylines and history
  • Rivalry and running-joke tracking
  • Trade and waiver analysis
  • FAAB activity analysis
  • Playoff race analysis
  • Weekly matchup previews
  • Commissioner announcements and trash talk
  • Season recaps and end-of-season awards
  • Support for multiple fantasy football platforms

Development Status

Fantasy Commissioner AI is currently an early-stage prototype being developed independently.

Initial development and testing is focused on authorized, read-only Yahoo Fantasy Football data.
